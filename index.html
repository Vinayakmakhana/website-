Grade Guide

Know your grade before you buy the sack

Makhana is sorted by seed size and whiteness right after roasting. Bigger, whiter pops go to retail and gifting; smaller grades suit grinding, kheer and industrial use. Exact specs are shared with a physical sample on request.

01
16–20mm

Special / Sonachandi

Largest, whitest pops. Premium retail packs, gifting boxes, roasted-whole snacking.

02
14–16mm

Grade 1

Even, well-formed pops. Standard retail packets and namkeen mixing.

03
12–14mm

Grade 2

Good for bulk roasting lines and flavoured snack manufacturing.

04
<12mm

Grade 3 / Mix

Smaller and broken pops. Suited to grinding, flour, and kheer preparations.

Grading terms can vary by mandi and region — tell us your use case and we'll match you to the right grade.
